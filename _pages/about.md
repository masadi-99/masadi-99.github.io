I'm a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Stanford, where I work in the [Ashley Lab](https://ashleylab.stanford.edu/), advised by Euan Ashley and co-advised by Fei-Fei Li and Ehsan Adeli. I build multimodal models for cardiology that read ECG, echocardiograms, and cardiac MRI, and I study where frontier multimodal models fail. In [MIRAGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21687), we found that they will confidently diagnose medical images they were never shown.

Before Stanford, I worked on human-motion generation at Samsung and on interpretable machine learning for education at EPFL, and I did my BSc in Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology.
